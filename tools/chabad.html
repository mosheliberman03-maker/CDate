Chabad.org English collector

Collects the English Chumash with Rashi and Tehillim from Chabad.org once, for the app.

  3. Go to chabad.org.
  4. Tap the address bar, type chen, and tap the chen bookmark that appears.

  6. A dark box appears at the bottom right away. If it stops, tap the bookmark again on chabad.org: it continues where it stopped. If the box shows a problem, send a screenshot.
