Install in a minute

The app isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you once to allow it.

Download

Tap Download for Android on this page. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway.

Open it

Tap the download in your notifications, or open Files → Downloads and tap KeepInTouch.apk.

Allow, then install

If asked, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.

Add the widget

Sign in and save your friends. Then long-press your home screen → Widgets → Keep in Touch.
